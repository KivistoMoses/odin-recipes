Grilled Fish with Jollof Rice


Ingredients

  • Fish
  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Vegetable oil
  • Black pepper
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Carrots or peas(optional)
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Lemon

    • Simple steps

    1. Season the fish with salt,pepper,garlic,thyme, and other prefered spices.
    2. Grill the fish untill it is golden brown and fully cooked.
    3. Prepare the sauce: fry onions, add tomato paste and blended tomatoes and pepper, then cook untill the sauce thickens.
    4. Add the rice and enough water or stock. Season and allow it to cook untill the rice is tender.
    5. Add vegetables such as carrots or peasif desired.
    6. Serve the jollof rice with the grilled fish and lemon slices.
