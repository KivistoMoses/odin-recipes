Grilled Chicken with vegetables


Ingredients:

  • Chicken breast or Chicken leg
  • Green peas
  • Carrots
  • Cabbage
  • Onion
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Tomato sauce

Simple steps:

  1. Season the chicken with salt,pepper and garlic.
  2. Grill or fry the chicken over medium heat untill golden brown and fully cooked.
  3. Prepare the vegetables: saute chopped onion,carrots and cabbage in a little oil.
  4. Cook the peas separately untill tender, then add them to the vegetables.
  5. Prepare a simple source with tomto with your prefered seasoning if desired.
  6. Serve: place the chicken on a plate with the vegetables and peas, then add the sauce.
