Eru and Fufu

Ingredients

  • Eru leaves
  • Waterleaf or spinach
  • Beef or assorted meat
  • Smoked fish or dried fish
  • Palm oil
  • Crayfish
  • Smoked fish or dried fish
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Pepper

Simple steps:

  1. wash and cut the eru and Waterleaf.
  2. Boil the meat and fish with onion, salt and seasoning.
  3. Add Waterleaf and allow it to cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add eru leaves, Crayfish and pepper.
  5. Pour in palm oil and mix everything well.
  6. Prepare your fufu separately and serve it with the eru.
