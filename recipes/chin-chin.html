Chin-Chin

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • suger
  • Milk
  • Butter or magarine
  • Eggs
  • Baking powser
  • Nutmeng
  • A little salt
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Simple steps:

  1. Mix the dry ingredients: Flour,suger,baking powder,nutmeg and salt.
  2. Add butter,mutter, and eggs and mix into a firm dough.
  3. Roll out the dough on a lightly floured surface.
  4. Cut the dough into small squares or desired shapes.
  5. Heat the oil and deep-fry the pieces in batches.
  6. Fry untill golden brown, then remove and allow them to cool and become crispy.
